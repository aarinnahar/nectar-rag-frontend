🔍 Deep-Dive Performance Insights

Node: analyze_metrics_node
01 // Winner Rationale 🏆

Recursive Splitter Dominates

Recursive splitter wins with 57.4% score and 0.525 recall, maintaining strong structural integrity. Its cost efficiency at $3.162 per 1k queries and balanced recall make it the most viable option, outperforming others in both quality and economics.

Comparative Delta

By leveraging recursive chunking, the splitter preserves semantic boundaries, boosting recall to 0.525 while keeping token count low. This yields a 57.4% score and a $3.162 cost per 1k queries, outperforming the character and token splitters in both accuracy and efficiency.

02 // Runner-Up Rationale 🥈

Character Splitter Nears Top

Character splitter secures runner‑up with 56.1% score and 0.475 recall. Its throughput remains competitive, and cost per 1k queries is $4.074, but minor structural boundary trade‑offs limit its precision, keeping it behind the recursive splitter.

Comparative Delta

The character splitter balances recall and cost, yet its rigid chunking introduces slight boundary misalignments that reduce precision. At $4.074 per 1k queries, it trails the recursive splitter by 1.3 percentage points in score, underscoring the impact of structural health on overall performance.

03 // Loser Rationale 📉

Token Splitter Fails Retrieval

Token splitter lags with a 46.2% score and a recall of 0.6, repeatedly missing essential sentences. Its high token bloat and weak boundary health degrade retrieval quality, making it unsuitable for deployment.

Comparative Delta

Despite reasonable throughput, the token splitter's excessive tokenization inflates context size, causing frequent boundary misalignments and critical retrieval failures. This results in a 46.2% index score and a recall of 0.6, highlighting its inefficiency.
